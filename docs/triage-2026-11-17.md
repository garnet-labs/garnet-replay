## Reproducible review boundary

The manifest holds receipt-bound operator assessments, not hostname rules.
Changing the head, compared commit, scope or any receipt byte invalidates an
assessment. A finalized trusted App receipt is required. Missing, trimmed,
unreadable, partial or unbound evidence stays undeterminable. Readable changed
records without an assessment stay needs-review.

Use `--no-verify` only for internal triage; it never marks a row shareable.
Sharing requires a current-head PASS and either a supported finding or scoped
quiet control. Cards and cohort rows retain the original recorded job and
background execution sections alongside the review classification.
